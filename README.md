# 🩺 GENESIS

### Integrated Digital Platform for Diabetes Diagnosis & Monitoring

A full-stack healthcare web application designed to support **early diabetes diagnosis** and **long-term glucose monitoring** through secure digital records, AI-assisted clinical interpretation, and interactive health analytics. The platform replaces fragmented paper-based reports with a centralized patient management system, enabling both patients and clinicians to make faster, data-driven decisions.

---

## ✨ Features

- Secure patient registration and role-based authentication
- Digital storage of laboratory reports and blood glucose records
- AI-assisted classification of **Normal, Prediabetic, and Diabetic** status using ADA/WHO guidelines
- Longitudinal monitoring of fasting glucose, post-prandial glucose, and HbA1c trends
- Interactive dashboard with graphical visualization of diabetic parameters
- Report date verification to ensure authenticity and prevent data tampering
- Medication and prescription record management
- REST API-based architecture with encrypted database support

## 🎯 Project Impact

GENESIS aims to improve non-communicable disease care by enabling systematic diabetes screening, reducing diagnostic delays, strengthening treatment adherence, and providing clinicians with validated longitudinal patient data for evidence-based decision making.
